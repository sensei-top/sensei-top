Sensei Top and Untapped

The two answer different questions. This page says where each one is the better fit. What it says about Untapped is what Untapped's own pages, its companion and Premium, said on 2 November 2026.

Where Sensei Top is the better fit

  • Your whole history, free. Every feature is in the free download today, and every game it has read stays in your history. Untapped's profile history covers the last two sets on its free tier and up to two years on Premium, and its global best-of-three data is Premium only.
  • Magic Online. The same tracker reads Magic Online's game logs into the same dashboard. Untapped's companion is an Arena app. Sensei Top for Magic Online
  • Linux and the Steam Deck. Untapped's companion is for Windows and Mac. Sensei Top runs on Windows and on Linux, the Steam Deck's desktop mode included. Sensei Top on Linux
  • No account, and your matches stay on your computer. The dashboard runs on your own machine and answers only to it. A match of yours leaves it only if you set a Claude key of your own and press a generate button, which sends that report's prompt to Anthropic under your key. The privacy page lists every request the app makes.
  • The field from tournaments. The field comes from real tournament results, tiered by how much evidence stands behind each share, and each win rate shows how many games stand behind it.

What Untapped does that Sensei Top does not

  • An overlay. Untapped draws over the game. Sensei Top shows the match in its own window for now; an overlay for Arena is on the roadmap.
  • Draft. Untapped rates draft picks. Sensei Top does not cover draft, and draft is not planned.
  • A Mac app. Sensei Top has no build for macOS yet.

Which one to use

For an overlay today, or for help in a draft, Untapped is the one. For constructed on Arena or Magic Online, with every number free and every game on your own computer, try Sensei Top. It only reads Arena's log and never touches the game, so it runs beside any other tracker, and you can try both.
