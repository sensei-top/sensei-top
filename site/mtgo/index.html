Sensei Top for Magic Online

Magic Online writes a log of every game you play. Sensei Top reads those files and builds your match history from them, on the same dashboard as Arena's, on Windows or under Wine on Linux.

What it reads

The Match_GameLog files Magic Online writes for each match, and the decks it keeps beside them, plus any .dek files you export to a folder of your own. It only reads them: nothing Magic Online owns is ever changed, and nothing reads the client's memory or plays for you.

What it needs from you

Magic Online's log never says which player is you, so the app asks for your name on Magic Online and the folder of game logs, in its welcome step or later in Settings, under Sources. On Windows, when it finds the folder Magic Online is writing, it fills it in for you. The log never names your deck either, so a match counts toward one of your decks once you pick it: open the match and answer Which deck was this?, or confirm a whole group from the Needs a deck card on Home. The help page has the details.

What you get

  • Every match and game, with a report for each match and each game's plays, turn by turn.
  • Your record with each deck and against each archetype, with the number of games behind every rate.
  • The field from real Magic Online and paper tournament results, with your own record against each archetype in it.
  • The coach and the leak finder, across Arena and Magic Online alike.

The Live page, during a game, is Arena's only for now. Magic Online's log records plays and no board, so a game has no board and no charts, and the parts of a report that need one say what is missing.
