The rules coach

Not available yet. Applies once the rules coach launches.

  • It is an AI and can be wrong. Every answer carries this line: "AI answer, may be wrong. Check the cited rules; at a tournament, ask a judge." Read the cited rules before you rely on an answer.
  • It is not a judge. An answer is not an official ruling. At a tournament, the judge's ruling is the one that counts. Wizards of the Coast does not write, check or endorse the coach's answers.
  • What it answers. Magic rules and card interactions, in English. It declines other questions, and a declined question still counts toward your daily limit. A question can be up to 500 characters long.
  • Where your question goes. Answers are written by Anthropic's Claude models. Anthropic stores your question in the United States and may process it in other countries. The coach asks you to agree to that the first time you use it; the Privacy Policy explains what is sent.
  • Limits. Each player can ask 10 questions a day; the count resets at 00:00 UTC. You may need to wait a few seconds between questions. To keep the coach free, it also has a shared monthly budget: when that budget, or the day's share of it, runs out, the coach pauses for everyone until it resets. We may also pause it at any time to fix a problem. A paused coach does not count your question.
  • The limits may change. We may change them, for example after the beta. We will show the current limits in the app and on the website.
