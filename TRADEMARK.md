by that notice. The project's maintainer uses them as unregistered
trademarks (Sensei Top™), and they exist to tell people which software

This page says what you may do without asking.

## You may

- Say in words that your work is **built on Sensei Top**, **based on Sensei Top**
  or **compatible with Sensei Top**.
- Use the name to refer to this project: in an article, a video, a review,
  a comparison, a bug report.

## You may not

- Name your own software or service "Sensei Top", or anything close
  enough to be confused with it.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests this project endorses, runs
  or answers for your version, your service or your product.
- Use the logo as the icon of your own application, or register the name
  or one like it as a domain, an account or a trademark.

## Why

Someone who installs a program called Sensei Top should get what this
project published, with the privacy promises this project made.
